# Retired SSEBop source probe

The files here record the initial daily-source and raster-grid probes. They are
not read by the experiment. Before any model result was generated, the active
ET constraint was changed to the official monthly 2015-2018 series to make the
streamed, checksummed extraction tractable. Active files are under
`data/derived/ssebop_monthly/` and `provenance/ssebop/`.
