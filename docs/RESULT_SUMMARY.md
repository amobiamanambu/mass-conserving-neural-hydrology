# Audited result summary

The final analysis includes four closure-admitted architectures and three seeds.
National median KGE is 0.606 for Hybrid-SHM, 0.600 for dHBV, 0.590 for the flux
gate, and 0.478 for MCP-MA3. Pairwise response claims are therefore conditioned
on gauges where both models reach the stated skill threshold rather than on
similar national medians.

At KGE >= 0.60, Hybrid-SHM's seven-day response differs from dHBV and the flux
gate under both intervention designs. With zero PET, the cross-architecture gaps
are 2.36 and 1.35 times the largest within-architecture seed gap. With PET held
at climatology, they are 2.27 and 1.45 times that seed reference. Hybrid-SHM has
the higher response at 83-92% of mutually qualified gauges under the
climatological-PET sensitivity.

The other climatological-PET pairwise gaps are near within-architecture
variability and lack a consistent directional separation. This convergence is
part of the result: response disagreement is not inevitable, but exact mass
conservation and useful streamflow prediction do not guarantee invariance.

MCP-MA3's zero-PET probe yields rising flow at 41.2% of gauges. Its large
zero-PET gaps are therefore treated as a probe-specific mechanism rather than
the basis of the central claim.
